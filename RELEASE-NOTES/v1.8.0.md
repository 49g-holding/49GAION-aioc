# 49GAION AIOC definition and template v1.8.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Released with the hub's 49GAION Standard `v1.8.0` and the 49GAION Operating Protocol `v1.8.0`. Their decisions are the hub's records [`DR-0032`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0032-withdrawn-aion-builder-stages-delivery-test.md) to [`DR-0043`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0043-the-49gaion-guide-version-1.md), each listed with its subject in the hub's [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md). Released on the governing seat's word of 2026-10-25 (translated): "I agree with all of it. Do all of it."

## Summary

- **Withdrawn** (`49GAION/DR-0032`): the AION Builder and the delivery test. `docs/WHAT-IS-AN-AIOC.md` §7 drops the sentence on the handover; the template's guidance on the Type drops the sentence on the AION Builder. The stop door and the rules that stay in force are unchanged.
- **Live** (`49GAION/DR-0037`): a blueprint filled from the template is a starting point for the organisation's own `[ORG]AION`, which designs and configures the AIOC inside the organisation and keeps it current; never a finished configuration made elsewhere and installed as it is.
- **Readings of the complete definition** (`49GAION/DR-0041`), in `docs/WHAT-IS-AN-AIOC.md`, `docs/STANDARD-CAPABILITIES.md` and the template: the panel is the organisation's own interface, written in code, from which every task of the unit can be controlled; the back office, the AI chat workspace, is an authorised interface too and keeps the panel; the hub of the operation, human and AI, is the Top Management Group, the first AIOC under the core, and wherever a text names `[ORG]AIOC-OCO`, read the Top Management Group or a separate `[ORG]AIOC-OCO` where the organisation has one. The README points to the definition; where this repository differs from it, the definition governs until the text is rewritten.
- **The Chief of Staff** (`49GAION/DR-0042`): it may order and summarise what reaches its human position; it never drops or delays any of it; the raw record is always open to that position; the protected route of a report, an objection or a stop never passes through any AI.
- **The version.** The version labels, the template version and `state.json` move to `v1.8.0`.
- **The Protocol pin.** `PROTOCOL-PIN` moves to the Operating Protocol `2dc78db`, the commit from which the Protocol `v1.8.0` is released; its schemas are those of the Protocol `v1.8.0`.
