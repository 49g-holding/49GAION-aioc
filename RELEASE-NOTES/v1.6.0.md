# 49GAION AIOC definition and template v1.6.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: twenty records of the hub, [`DR-0008`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0008-source-lifecycle-first-stage.md) to [`DR-0027`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0027-operations-event-register.md), each listed with its subject in [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md). The rows of `CHANGELOG.md` marked `v1.6.0` give each change in full.

## Summary

- Mechanisms from practice in the standard capabilities (DR-0010):
  - Office: the windowed position;
  - Chief of Staff: the delegation ladder, completed staff work, the duty deputy and the holder Chief of Staff;
  - Input socket: the readiness verdict, and no authority from writable files;
  - Panel: a chat as a Panel view, the state snapshot, Health in three states;
  - Cells and Pods: assignment;
  - the template: the mechanism register and the Reserve.
- Every human-pointing card field takes a position code only. A seat has an arrangement: Solo, Team or Overseen (DR-0011, DR-0012).
- Two positions are two parties, and a human is present as its position (DR-0015).
- From the Library's design stages (DR-0019, DR-0021):
  - a Library mother is named by pin;
  - the stages table, for an AIOC whose work runs in stages;
  - the Library's cases mapped to the AIOC's own;
  - a **Done when** row in every capability's Definition;
  - `docs/WRITING-REPOSITORY.md`, what a writing repository holds and the checks it runs.
- `PROTOCOL-PIN` moves to the Operating Protocol `v1.6.0` content. The template's card blocks validate there with no finding.

## Checks on main

position-fields is added, not required at first. It checks that every human-pointing field holds a position code, and that each arrangement's structure is complete.

## Known limits

- The capability texts carry their fixed-test cases in words; no machine test runs them yet.
