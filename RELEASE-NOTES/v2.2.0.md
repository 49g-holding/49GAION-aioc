# 49GAION AIOC definition and template v2.2.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Released with the hub's 49GAION Standard `v2.2.0` and the 49GAION Operating Protocol `v2.2.0`. Decision: the hub's record [`DR-0053`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0053-the-review-rooms-designs-v2-2-0.md), item 11, on the governing seat's words of 29 October 2026 (translated from Persian): "It can give its Chief of Staff full access on its own behalf, even to sign. But that does not mean that AI decides."

## Summary

- **A human position works with AI through its own Chief of Staff,** and may give it any authority it holds: from preparing and approving each step to full access on its behalf, signing for it included, a type of the Human Floor signature list included. **That never means AI decides:** whatever the Chief of Staff does under its grant is the position's own decision (Cells and Pods §1 item 9; What is an AIOC, item 5; Standard Capabilities).
- **Each position grants only to its own Chief of Staff,** with a fresh live sign-in, per action or standing, revocable at once. AI does not give to AI: a Chief of Staff passes access, budget or authority to another AI part only with its position's leave, written in its grant. No other attached Cell signs for a position.
- **The delegation ladder** never gives the right to sign; only the position's grant does.
- **The template** is unchanged and stays `v2.1.0`. **`PROTOCOL-PIN`** stays at the Operating Protocol's release `v2.1.0` until the Protocol's release `v2.2.0` is tagged.
- The wording of the rules that was superseded is listed at the end of each text, verbatim, under `v2.2.0`.
