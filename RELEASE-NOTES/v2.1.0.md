## Summary

- **Positions only** (`49GAION/DR-0048`, `DR-0049`): an AIOC knows two kinds of actor, AI and human positions; who holds a position, and how, is the organisation's own mechanism, outside the system.
  - **The template.** The accountable seat is a position; the work table has no Deputy column, and "Run today by" names an AI agent or a human position; a personal signature with no answer stays held and is raised; attached Cells name the Cell of the position they serve; no `decision_rule`; Annex A.3 says Cover in place of Deputy.
  - **The texts.** The Position Chief of Staff (renamed from Holder Chief of Staff); the Position Principle; the Overseen link in place of the arrangement models Solo and Team; the delegated-act tag; the veto. A HOAC label is held by one position (`label_holder`).
  - **The checks** read only the Overseen link between positions.
  - The superseded wording is listed at the end of each text, verbatim.
- **The version.** The version labels, the template version and `state.json` move to `v2.1.0`.
- **The Protocol pin.** `PROTOCOL-PIN` moves to the Operating Protocol `v2.1.0`; the filled template validates against its unit card schema.

## Known limits

- **Nothing has run yet.** Every blueprint filled from the template is a starting point; every fixed test of every part reads `NOT_RUN` until the part is built and run.
