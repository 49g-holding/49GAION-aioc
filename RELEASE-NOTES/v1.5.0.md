# 49GAION AIOC definition and template v1.5.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: [`DR-0006`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0006-decision-record-lock.md) (decision records frozen; how a change reaches main) and [`DR-0007`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0007-49gaion-v1-5-0-structure-accountability-signatures.md) (structure, accountability, signatures, gateway and register).

## Summary

- This repository moves from v1.3.0 directly to v1.5.0 to align with the Standard; there is no v1.4.0 of the AIOC definition. The next release of this repository must be above v1.5.0.
- The AIOC definition, Cells and Pods, and standard capabilities follow the Standard v1.5.0, including the Chief of Staff rule: whatever the filler, a Chief of Staff never signs for its person.
- The blueprint template is filled to the v1.5.0 unit card schema and passes template-fill with no known warnings.

## Checks on main

Required checks gain github-separation, ci-selftest and template-fill, which now fails on every finding. The no-org-names check also judges added or changed lines against a private list of person names, reporting path and line only and failing closed.

## Known limits

- The person-name check proves its given-name variant forms only through fictional selftest cases; no run on real text exercises those forms.
