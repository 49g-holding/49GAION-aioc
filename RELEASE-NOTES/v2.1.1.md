## Summary

- **The Chief of Staff signs only with its position's grant.** Each human position sets up its own Chief of Staff and may grant it up to the right to sign for it; within that grant it signs, tagged as a delegated act. An attached Cell, a Chief of Staff included, never signs a type of the Human Floor signature list (Cells and Pods §1 item 9; What is an AIOC; Standard Capabilities).
- **Top Cell.** A label is held by one position; a rule that needs several signers names its signing positions (Cells and Pods).
- **The Protocol pin.** `PROTOCOL-PIN` names the Operating Protocol's release tag `v2.1.0`.
- **The template** is unchanged and stays `v2.1.0`.
- The wording of the rules that was superseded is listed at the end of each text, verbatim, under `v2.1.1`; corrections of a link, a label or a version are not listed.
